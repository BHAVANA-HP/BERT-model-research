Lumina Systems Leave Policy
Version 3.2, effective 1 April 2026

1. Annual leave: Every full-time employee gets 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
2. Sick leave: 10 days per year. A medical certificate is needed for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.
3. Carry forward: Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried into the next year. The rest lapse on 31 December.
4. Parental leave: 26 weeks for the primary caregiver and 2 weeks for the other parent.
5. Applying for leave: Submit requests in the HR portal at least 5 working days ahead, except for sick leave and emergencies.
6. Approval: Your reporting manager approves leave within 2 working days.
