Lumina Systems Expense Reimbursement Policy
Version 1.5, effective 1 April 2026

1. Submit claims within 30 days of the expense, with receipts attached.
2. Meals while travelling: up to Rs 1,500 per day.
3. Hotels: up to Rs 6,000 per night in metro cities and Rs 4,000 elsewhere.
4. Any single expense above Rs 10,000 needs approval from your department head before you spend it.
5. Alcohol, personal entertainment and fines are not reimbursed.
6. Claims are paid in the next monthly payroll after approval.
