Project Update: Q3 Cloud Cost Review
Prepared by: Chen Wei (chen.wei@luminasystems.example)
Date: 30 September 2026

- Total cloud spend for Q3 was Rs 18.4 lakh, up 12% from Q2.
- The increase came from the Atlas staging servers and larger storage for the Orchid migration.
- About 20% of compute instances ran idle outside working hours.

Recommendations:
- Shut down staging servers automatically at night and on weekends.
- Buy reserved instances for the three servers that run all year.
- Review storage every month and delete old snapshots.
