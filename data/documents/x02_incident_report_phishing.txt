Security Incident Report IR-2026-014
Date: 20 September 2026
Reported by: Neha Kulkarni
Handled by: Imran Sheikh, IT Support

Summary:
A phishing email pretending to be from the "Lumina IT Billing Team" was sent from billing@lumina-systems-support.example to 40 employees. It asked staff to log in to a fake payroll page hosted at IP address 203.0.113.45.

Impact:
- 3 employees clicked the link. 1 entered their password.
- The password was reset within 30 minutes. No data was lost.

Actions:
- The sender domain was blocked.
- All staff were reminded to report suspicious emails to security@luminasystems.example or the hotline on +91 80 4000 1299.
- Phishing awareness training will be held in October.
