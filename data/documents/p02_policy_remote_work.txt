Lumina Systems Hybrid and Remote Work Policy
Version 2.0, effective 1 June 2026

1. Hybrid model: Employees work from the office 3 days a week and may work remotely 2 days a week. Teams choose their office days together.
2. Core hours: Everyone must be reachable between 11:00 and 16:00 IST.
3. Equipment: The company provides a laptop. Personal laptops must not be used for client work.
4. Connection: Use the company VPN whenever you access internal systems from home.
5. Workspace: Work from a quiet place where screens cannot be seen by others during client calls.
6. Full-time remote work needs written approval from your department head and HR.
