Metropolitan Athletic Club: Fitness Center Amenities and Classes

Welcome to Metropolitan Athletic Club, your premier downtown fitness destination designed to support your active lifestyle. Our newly renovated facility features two full floors of premium resistance training machines, olympic lifting platforms, custom dumbbells up to one hundred pounds, and dedicated cardiovascular equipment including treadmills, rowing machines, and stair climbers equipped with personal video displays. Members enjoy unlimited access to over fifty group exercise classes each week, ranging from high-intensity interval training (HIIT) and indoor cycling to restorative vinyasa yoga. Our certified personal trainers offer customized workout routines, body composition assessments, and technique coaching. After your training session, unwind in our luxury locker rooms complete with cedarwood dry saunas, steam rooms, plush towel service, and a smoothie bar offering organic protein shakes and pre-workout refreshments.
