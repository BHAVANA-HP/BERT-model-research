Preparing for a Screening Colonoscopy: A Guide for Patients

A screening colonoscopy is an essential diagnostic endoscopic examination that evaluates the mucosal lining of the large intestine and rectum for precancerous polyps and inflammatory lesions. Effective procedural visualization requires complete bowel preparation starting the day prior to the appointment. Patients consume a clear liquid diet and drink split-dose polyethylene glycol lavage solutions to evacuate stool contents thoroughly. During the procedure, the gastroenterologist administers moderate or deep sedation to ensure patient comfort while advancing a flexible colonoscope equipped with a high-definition camera. Any identified adenomatous polyps are excised using endoscopic mucosal resection techniques and submitted for histopathological biopsy, effectively preventing progression to colorectal carcinoma.
