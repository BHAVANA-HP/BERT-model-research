What to Expect During Your Annual Cardiology Consultation

An annual cardiology clinic visit begins with a comprehensive review of the patient medical history, familial cardiovascular risk factors, and current prescription medications. A clinical nurse records baseline vital signs, including seated blood pressure in both arms, resting pulse, oxygen saturation, and body mass index. The attending cardiologist performs a thorough physical examination, utilizing a stethoscope to detect potential heart murmurs, carotid bruits, or abnormal lung crackles. A standard resting twelve-lead electrocardiogram (ECG) is routinely conducted to assess cardiac electrical conduction and detect signs of prior ischemia or arrhythmias. Based on clinical findings, the specialist may order diagnostic blood panels for lipid profiles and troponin, or schedule non-invasive imaging such as an echocardiogram or treadmill exercise stress test.
