All-Star Point Guard Sidelined with Ankle Sprain in Game 4 Defeat

The Chicago Bulls suffered a devastating blow to their championship playoff ambitions during last night Game 4 clash against the Miami Heat when starting All-Star point guard Marcus Vance exited the court in the third quarter. Attempting to contest a contested corner three-pointer, Vance landed awkwardly on a defender foot, turning his right ankle severely before collapsing onto the hardwood in visible distress. The team head athletic trainer and medical staff rushed out immediately, assisting Vance directly into the locker room for diagnostic x-rays and clinical examination. Team officials confirmed this morning that an MRI revealed a Grade 2 lateral ankle ligament sprain. While surgery will not be required, Vance is officially ruled out for Game 5 and faces a projected rehabilitation timeline of two to three weeks, forcing the coaching staff to restructure their starting backcourt rotation.
