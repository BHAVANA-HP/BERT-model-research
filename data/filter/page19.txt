Decadent Homemade Salted Caramel Fudge: The Secret to Smooth Texture

Making old-fashioned confectionery fudge at home is an art that hinges on precise temperature control and sugar crystallization management. This rich recipe combines granulated white sugar, dark brown sugar for molasses depth, heavy whipping cream, sweet cream butter, and golden corn syrup in a heavy-bottomed saucepan. Bring the mixture to a steady rolling boil over medium heat without stirring, attaching a calibrated candy thermometer to the side of the pan. When the syrup registers exactly 238 degrees Fahrenheit—the classic soft-ball confectionery stage—remove the pot from the heat immediately. Allow the molten sugar mixture to cool to lukewarm before beating vigorously with a wooden spoon until the glossy sheen turns matte, then pour into a parchment-lined baking tin and sprinkle flaky sea salt over the top.
