Understanding Amoxicillin: Dosage and Indications

Amoxicillin is a moderate-spectrum penicillin antibiotic widely prescribed to treat various bacterial infections, including acute otitis media, streptococcal pharyngitis, skin infections, and lower respiratory tract illnesses. It functions by inhibiting bacterial cell wall synthesis, leading to lysis of susceptible dividing bacteria. Standard adult dosages typically range from 250 mg to 500 mg administered every eight hours, or 500 mg to 875 mg every twelve hours, depending on the infection severity and renal clearance. Common adverse reactions include mild nausea, diarrhea, and transient skin rash. Healthcare providers emphasize that patients must complete the entire course of prescribed antibacterial therapy even if symptoms resolve early, as premature discontinuation encourages the emergence of antibiotic-resistant bacterial strains.
