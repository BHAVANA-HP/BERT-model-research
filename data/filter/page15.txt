Authentic Neapolitan Pizza Dough: Technique and Fermentation

Crafting authentic Neapolitan pizza dough at home requires patience, simple pantry ingredients, and precise hydration calculations. The classic formula calls for Italian Type 00 high-protein wheat flour, water at roughly 65% hydration, fine sea salt, and a minuscule quantity of active dry yeast. After combining the ingredients and kneading the mass until a smooth gluten windowpane develops, the dough undergoes an extended bulk fermentation in a cool environment for twenty-four to forty-eight hours. This prolonged fermentation develops complex, slightly tangy lactic flavors and creates an airy, extensible crumb. When baked in a specialized pizza oven reaching 450 degrees Celsius, the dough produces a leopard-spotted charred crust and tender interior within ninety seconds.
