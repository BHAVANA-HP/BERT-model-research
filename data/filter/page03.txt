Dietary Guidelines for Managing Hypertension: The DASH Plan

The Dietary Approaches to Stop Hypertension (DASH) eating plan is a clinically validated nutritional strategy designed to lower elevated systolic and diastolic blood pressure. The diet emphasizes fresh vegetables, fruits, whole grains, lean poultry, fish, and low-fat dairy products while significantly limiting saturated fats, red meats, and processed sodium. Potassium, magnesium, and calcium from natural dietary sources help counteract sodium retention and relax arterial walls. Clinical trials indicate that restricting daily sodium intake to less than 2,300 milligrams—and ideally down to 1,500 milligrams for individuals diagnosed with hypertension—can produce blood pressure reductions comparable to first-line antihypertensive pharmacotherapy. Registered dietitians advise hypertensive patients to inspect packaged food nutrition labels carefully and replace added salt with flavorful herbs and spices.
