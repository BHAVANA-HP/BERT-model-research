Premier League Weekend: Tactical Mastery in London Derby

Arsenal secured a vital three points in a dramatic 2-1 victory over Chelsea at Stamford Bridge on Sunday afternoon. After a tense, scoreless first half dominated by mid-block midfield pressing and cautious defending, the match burst into life in the 58th minute. The visiting manager shifted his tactical formation from a 4-3-3 to an aggressive 3-4-2-1, deploying wing-backs higher up the pitch to exploit space behind the Chelsea defense. The tactical gamble paid off immediately when an incisive low cross found the unmarked winger inside the penalty box for a composed side-foot finish into the bottom corner. Although Chelsea leveled ten minutes later from a towering set-piece header, a stoppage-time counterattack sealed Arsenal triumph, extending their lead at the summit of the league table.
